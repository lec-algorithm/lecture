## What it covers

- Graph representations: adjacency matrix and adjacency list
- BFS and DFS, and what each is good at
- Minimum spanning trees: greedy, Kruskal, Prim
- The min-heap and the priority queue
- Dijkstra's algorithm and the trouble with negative weights
- The A\* algorithm and heuristic functions
