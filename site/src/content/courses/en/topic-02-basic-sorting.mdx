## What it covers

- Selection sort: pick the smallest each round and move it to the front
- Insertion sort: slot each element into the sorted prefix
- How the two diverge in the best, average and worst cases
- Shell sort and bubble sort
- The two axes of sorting cost: comparisons and moves
